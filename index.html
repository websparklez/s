What I can build for you

I'll tell you early if you need less than you think. Plenty of people come to me asking for a big custom build when a well-made Wix site would do the job for a lot less money.

Wix websites

My usual pick for service businesses that want to edit their own site. You get a quick launch and an editor you can use without calling me every time you change a price.

Good fit if you run a service business and want to update text, photos and prices yourself.

Ask me about a Wix site

WordPress websites

Better than Wix when you need room to grow: a blog, booking tools, a members area or a lot of pages. I build with Elementor, so editing stays visual and you won't need to touch code.

Good fit if you expect the site to grow, or you want full control of your hosting.

Ask me about WordPress

Online stores

Product pages, categories and a checkout that works properly on phones, plus the delivery and returns details that make strangers comfortable paying you. I build stores on Wix or WordPress, depending on how much you sell.

Good fit if you sell physical or digital products and want orders coming in without chasing people in your DMs.

Ask me about a store

Local SEO

Service-area pages, proper page titles, your Google Business Profile and speed fixes, so people nearby find you before they find a competitor.

Good fit if you serve a town or region and most of your work comes from people searching close by.

Ask me about SEO

Custom coded websites

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript for when you want a very light, fast site with no monthly website-builder fee.

Good fit if page speed matters more to you than editing the site yourself.

Ask me about custom code

Redesigns in Figma

If your current site works but looks dated, I redesign it in Figma first. You see every page and ask for changes before any code gets written, which is a lot cheaper than changing things after launch.

Good fit if your site gets visitors but very few of them get in touch.

How a project with me usually goes

Timings are for a typical 5 to 8 page business site. Online stores take a bit longer.

  1. Day 1

    A quick chat

    You tell me about the business and who your customers are. I ask a lot of questions, then send you a fixed quote and a timeline.

  2. Week 1

    Plan and design

    I map out the pages and design your homepage in Figma. You see it and ask for changes before I build anything.

  3. Weeks 2 to 3

    Build

    I build the full site on a private preview link, so you can click around on your own phone and send me notes.

  4. Week 4

    SEO and launch

    Page titles, descriptions, speed fixes and Google Search Console go in, then we put the site live.

  5. After launch

    You're in charge

    I record a short walkthrough showing you how to edit your pages, and I'm a WhatsApp message away if anything breaks.

Every site I hand over comes with these

  • A layout designed for phones first, then tablets and desktops
  • Page titles, descriptions and structured data that Google can read
  • Google Search Console and Analytics, set up in your name
  • Compressed images and lean code, so pages load quickly
  • A WhatsApp button and contact form that reach you straight away
  • A short screen recording showing you how to edit your pages

Lighthouse scores I aim for on mobile

Lighthouse is Google's free tool for checking speed, accessibility, SEO and code quality. Wix limits how fast a page can get, so on Wix builds I'll tell you upfront what's realistic.

Questions people usually ask me first

Ask me a question

How much does a website cost?

It depends mostly on how many pages you need and whether you're selling online. After a short chat I send you a fixed quote, so you know the full cost before any work starts. If your budget is tight, tell me and I'll suggest what to build first.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical 5 to 8 page business site takes me about 3 to 4 weeks from our first chat to launch. Online stores take longer, mostly because of product setup. The biggest delay is usually waiting for content, so the sooner I have your text and photos, the sooner you go live.

Should I use WordPress or Wix?

For most small service businesses I'd go with Wix, because you can edit it yourself and hosting is taken care of. I'd pick WordPress if you need a bigger store, a busy blog or custom features, or if you want full control of your hosting. I'll tell you which one I think fits before you pay anything.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. I set every site up so you can change text, photos and prices without me, and I show you how before handover.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. I've built sites for clients in the UK, the US and Canada as well as here in Nigeria. We work over WhatsApp, email and video calls, and time zones haven't caused a problem so far.

Will my website show up on Google?

I set up the SEO basics on every build: page titles, descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and Google Search Console. Nobody can honestly promise you the top spot, but a fast, well-structured site gives you a much better chance than a slow one.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your logo, a rough idea of the pages you want and any photos you already have. If you don't have the words yet, I can help you write them.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, and it's often cheaper than starting over. I look at what's already working on your current site first, so we keep anything that brings in traffic.
